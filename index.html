Daily routine

Watching is continuous. Acting is on a slow, separate clock.

  1. Refresh quotes & scan the flags below
  2. A flag is a research prompt, never a buy order
  3. Before any trade, pass the four-question edge test
  4. Set the stop and target before you enter
  5. Log it, then check this curve against SPY
Edge test: What's the thesis? Why isn't it already in the price? What would prove me wrong? Over what horizon? If you can't answer #2, you have a guess, not an edge.
